Heating a small business building without guesswork

A company with a building of its own often has a heating system that grew over the years: an old boiler, a few split units, an office that is always too warm. One calculation brings it back into one plan.

Look at the whole building

Offices, a workshop and a storage space each need something else. An office wants steady warmth and fresh air, a workshop often needs less heat but more air, storage may need almost nothing. Treating the building as one space is how energy gets wasted.

Heating and cooling from one system

A heat pump can heat in winter and cool in summer. For many small business buildings that replaces two systems with one, and one contract for the maintenance.

One point of contact

What business owners tell us most is that they want one person to call. We do the calculation, the installation and the follow-up with our own team, and you keep the same contact from the first visit.

Where to start

Send us the floor plan and the energy bills of last year. We plan a visit, calculate the building and come back with one proposal.
