Questions we hear every week

Does a heat pump work in a house from the seventies?

Often it does, when the roof is insulated first and the radiators can deliver enough heat at a lower water temperature. The calculation tells you which of the two needs work.

Do I need system D ventilation with a heat pump?

Not always. In a house that becomes airtight after a renovation, balanced ventilation keeps the air healthy and keeps the heat inside. We tell you when it makes sense.

Can you help with the renovation premium?

We prepare the technical documents a premium application asks for. The application itself stays in your name.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on the building. You get a planning with the quote, and we keep to it.
